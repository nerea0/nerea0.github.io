**Electronic Health Records** (**EHR**) seem to be the hottest new way of storing patients' health-related information, from before birth to  after death [1]. Hovwever, they are not new at all as early efforts began on the 60s and 70s; back then, academica medical centers were developing what they called *clinical information systems*[2]. Later, this *clinical information systems* would become what we now know as EHR.
